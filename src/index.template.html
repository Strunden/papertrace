How it works

Your phone has no depth sensor it can use from a web page, so the app works out where the paper is by watching the printed marks on the canvas.

Why the canvas has those marks

Each printed mark is a square of known shape and position. Seeing a few of them gives the browser enough geometry to work out how the page is tilted and how far away it is - that is what lets your picture sit on the paper in correct perspective rather than floating on the screen. The canvas layout is known to the app, so the moment a few marks are in view the whole page locks instantly; after that, any single mark in view keeps it locked.

Getting steady results

  • Keep two or more marks in view when you can. One mark holds the lock, but small measurement errors grow with distance from it, so the picture can drift by a millimetre or two.
  • Don't cover the marks with your drawing hand - work from the side, or turn the page.
  • Even, diffuse light. Glare hides a mark; a hard shadow across one does too.
  • Print at 100% scale on plain matte paper - glossy stock reflects and blinds the tracker.
  • A phone stand or clamp helps enormously - not because the tracking needs it, but because your hands are then free to draw.

Trace styles

Neural models draw your photo entirely on your device (each style is a one-time download of 4-28 MB, then works offline). Artist sketch gives clean delicate lines, Rough sketch a loose underdrawing, Ink brush bold brush strokes. Paprika renders a painted version - a colour guide for painting what you traced. Ghost keeps the original photo, faded, for shading reference. Original shows it untouched - right for pictures that are already line art.

On your phone

The app lives at strunden.github.io/papertrace - open that link in Safari or Chrome. Add it to your home screen (Safari's Share sheet → Add to Home Screen): opening it that way drops the browser's own chrome and tab overhead, which noticeably smooths out the camera tracking.

Privacy

Everything runs locally in the browser - camera frames, your photos and the drawings made from them never leave the device. The only things ever downloaded are the app itself and the style models, each fetched once and cached for offline use.
