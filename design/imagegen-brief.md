## PROMPT

Instructional illustration for a mobile app that lets you trace a photo onto
real paper through your phone's camera.

**Scene (must be exactly this):** A printed sheet of paper lies on a warm
wooden desk, seen from a slightly elevated three-quarter view. The sheet has
eight small solid black squares around its border (four corners, four edge
midpoints) and one empty thin rectangular frame in the middle — the paper is
otherwise completely blank. A hand holds a smartphone flat above the sheet,
parallel to it, low enough that the screen is clearly a window onto the paper
below. On the phone screen we see the camera's view of that same sheet —
the black squares and the frame, perfectly lined up with the real sheet
around the phone — and inside the frame, on the screen only, sits a simple
line drawing of a daisy. The real paper visible around the phone shows no
daisy — it is blank except for one pencil line: a second hand with a pencil
is drawing a single petal on the real paper, exactly continuing a petal of
the on-screen daisy where it runs past the edge of the phone.

**The one idea the image must communicate:** the drawing exists only on the
screen; the paper is blank; the person is copying the screen's guide onto
the paper. If a viewer could mistake the daisy for being printed on the
paper, the image has failed.

**Accuracy rules:**
- The screen content must align with the sheet visible around the phone —
  same squares, same frame, continuing seamlessly, in a perspective
  consistent with the phone's position over the paper.
- The daisy appears ONLY on the screen (plus the single traced pencil petal
  on the paper).
- Eight squares total on the sheet; empty frame centered; nothing else
  printed on it.

**Style:** [YOUR STYLE HERE]

**Mood & colour:** warm, calm, crafted. Cream/warm-paper background world
(around #F4EEDE), dark warm ink, one or two accent colours max. Feels like a
beautifully printed manual page, inviting — "I could do this."

**Format:** 4:3 or square, generous margins, composition readable at
300 pixels wide.

**Do not include:** any text, lettering, labels, logos or watermarks; no UI
buttons on the phone screen; no extra flowers, no drawing on the paper other
than the single traced petal; no clutter on the desk.

---

## Suggested STYLE fills (pick one, or write your own)

1. **Modern editorial flat illustration** — confident flat shapes, minimal
   linework, subtle paper-grain texture, soft single-source shadow under the
   phone and hands; the style of a premium app's onboarding art or a New
   York Times explainer graphic.

2. **Risograph / screen-print poster** — two or three spot colours with
   slight misregistration, grainy ink coverage, bold silhouettes for the
   hands and phone, mid-century instructional-poster energy.

3. **Soft graphite & wash** — hand-drawn pencil linework with light
   watercolour-wash fills, visible paper tooth, the phone rendered cleanly
   so the "device vs. handmade world" contrast carries the concept.
